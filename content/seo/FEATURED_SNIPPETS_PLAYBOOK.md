## Engineering Checklists for Position-Zero Extraction

To guarantee Google’s parser extracts these blocks verbatim:
1. **Target Header Identity:** The `H2` or `H3` directly preceding the answer must mirror the natural search query:
   - Example: `## What is the Average SDE-2 Salary in Cyber City Gurugram?`
2. **First-Sentence Punch:** Place the answer within the first 15 words of the section.
3. **No Conversational Fluff:** Eliminate preambles such as *"In this section, we will analyze..."* or *"It depends on various factors..."*.
4. **Structured Table Tags:** Use valid Markdown table formatting with left-aligned or centered table headers. Ensure every row matches column counts without empty spans.
5. **Schema Integration:** Wrap these sections in JSON-LD `FAQPage` or `JobPosting` / `Occupation` schema in the page `<head>`.
